# Spotify Experiments to Improve User Experience

## Experiment 1: Enhanced Mood-Based Playlist Recommendation

### Description
Introduce a "Mood Mixer" feature where users select their current mood(s) via an interactive slider, and Spotify generates a tailored playlist dynamically combining moods like "Energetic," "Calm," or "Focused."

### Test Cells
- **Control Group (50%)**: Existing Discover Weekly and Daily Mix playlists only.
- **Test Group A (25%)**: Access to Mood Mixer with 3 predefined moods.
- **Test Group B (25%)**: Access to Mood Mixer with 6 granular mood options and mood-based podcast recommendations.

### Hypothesis
- **Leading Metrics**: Increased playlist starts, session length.
- **Lagging Metrics**: Higher retention rate, increased daily active users, improved user satisfaction scores.

---

## Experiment 2: Social Listening Party Feature

### Description
Allow users to create synchronized group listening sessions with friends remotely, including chat and emoji reactions within the app.

### Test Cells
- **Control Group (60%)**: Current listening features without social party.
- **Test Group (40%)**: Access to Social Listening Party with chat and reactions.

### Hypothesis
- **Leading Metrics**: Increase in session duration, number of shared playlists.
- **Lagging Metrics**: Growth in collaborative playlist creation, increased social shares, improved Net Promoter Score (NPS).

---

## Experiment 3: Smart Offline Download Suggestions

### Description
Auto-download songs and podcasts for offline listening based on user’s historical behavior and calendar events (e.g., commute time, travel).

### Test Cells
- **Control Group (70%)**: Manual offline downloads only.
- **Test Group (30%)**: Auto-download suggestions with opt-in notifications.

### Hypothesis
- **Leading Metrics**: Number of offline downloads, frequency of offline listening.
- **Lagging Metrics**: User engagement during offline mode, reduction in drop-off during poor connectivity, increased subscription renewal rate.
